Motor Specifications

T-Motor Velox V2207 V3 2550KV

KV: 2550KV (RPM per volt)
Weight: ~36.5g
Shaft: 5mm
Voltage: 4S
Max Current: ~41.8A
Max Power: ~642W
Idle Current (10V): ~2.25A

Estimated RPM:
RPM ≈ KV × Voltage
≈ 2550 × 14.8 ≈ 37,700 RPM (no load)

Designed for 5-inch FPV drones
Higher KV = faster response and more aggressive flight behavior
Requires a battery with a high C rating
A 45A ESC is enough for this motor
