Here are some tools you will definetely need for this project, and some optional ones that can be really useful, but not necessary, you can always get them later if you want to.

Solder
This is the most important tool you will need, you will need it to solder all the components together, and to make sure that everything is connected properly, you can get a cheap soldering iron for this project, but make sure that it has a fine tip so you can work with small components, also make sure to get some good quality solder and flux, it will make your life much easier and will help you to avoid cold joints and other soldering issues.

I recommend you to avoid lead-free solder, it is more difficult to work with and it can cause more issues, especially for beginners, so just get some good quality leaded solder and you will be good to go.

get a 63/37 stain for better results

Flux, this is very important, it helps to clean the surfaces and to improve the flow of the solder, it will make your soldering much easier and will help you to avoid cold joints and other issues, just get a good quality flux and you will see the difference. Many people say this is optional but i think it is really important, especially for beginners, so don't skip it.

For the iron you need at least 60w, in the esc there are some big pads that require a lot of heat to solder properly, if you have a low wattage iron it will be very difficult to solder those pads and you will end up with cold joints and other issues, so just get a good quality 60w soldering iron and you will be good to go. 

Get a soldering sucker to, so you can easily remove solder

Multimeter, this is a very useful tool for testing and troubleshooting your drone, it will help you to check the continuity of your connections, to measure the voltage and current, and to diagnose any issues that may arise during the build process, you can get a cheap multimeter for this project, but make sure that it has a good range and that it is easy to use.

You can see if something is shorting or if there is a bad connection, you can also use it to check the voltage of your battery and to make sure that everything is working properly before you fly your drone, no need for a very good or expensive just a cheap one solve most of the problems

Cutting tools, you need some of them to cut wires, strip it, and don't have any difficulty that can late your project, you can get a wire stripper, it will make your life much easier when it comes to cutting and stripping wires, also make sure to get some good quality wire cutters, they will help you to cut wires cleanly and without fraying, and you can also get some scissors for cutting heat shrink tubing and other materials.

Tweezers, here is a simple thing that helps you don't burn your fingers when you are soldering small components, it will help you to hold the components in place while you are soldering them.

Heat shrink tubing, this is a very useful tool for insulating your connections and for protecting them from damage, it comes in different sizes and colors, you can get a variety pack of heat shrink tubing for this project, it will give you a good selection of sizes and colors to work with, also make sure to get a good quality heat shrink tubing that is made of durable material and that has a good adhesive, it will help to keep your connections secure and protected.

Zip ties, those are really useful for organizing your wires and for keeping everything neat and tidy, you can get a variety pack of zip ties for this project, it will give you a good selection of sizes and colors to work with, also make sure to get some good quality zip ties that are made of durable material and that have a good locking mechanism, it will help to keep your wires secure and organized. Those stupdly cheap and help so much

Isopropyl alcohol, You will need this to clean your board from flux and other residues, just make sure the board isn't powered when you are cleaning because sparks can set the whole thing in fire, get a old toothbrush and some cotton and you are good to go

Microscope, this is optional but very good in case you have issues when looking at tiny things, it will help you not forcing your eyes.
