Chosing the receiver was not very hard since it just needs to match the protocol your RC works on so since my RC works on ERLC i grabbed the Ep1 Rx 2.4g Expresslrs Elrs Nano, it's tiny, simple and can at least when buyed it, it camed with some big flexible antennas and i spread it all over the frame so i don't need to worry about good angle because i'm using omnidirecional sending antenna

Omnidirectional antennas:

  These spread the signal in all directions, so you don’t need to worry too much about orientation, but are less powerful.
  
Bidirectional antenna:

These are very powerful and can reach ranges that omni never could but you need to point it right in the receiving antenna

Why chosse ELRS

Because it's a modern open-source protocol that's has extremely low latency and it's world supported

ELRS is one of the best choices for modern FPV builds, especially for reliability and range.
