For the RC controller, it was a bit hard to find a really good one because those I used to find were just expensive and overkill with a lot of functions I would never use, I found some really cheap one but it was just an RC toy looking like professional.

I was searching preferably for RadioMaster(Brand) then I found the RadioMaster pocket, it was a really good one with the ELRS protocol that is modern and reliable so I choose this one

Here are some info about it

RF protocol

It uses the ExpressLRS technology that is based in LoRa which stands for Long Range, it's know for it's low latência and high quality of signal, consequently turning into the most used protocol in fpv and drones in general

It's energy consume from the controller is most of the times less than 250miliWatts 

it's frequency it's 2.4ghz allowing high band rate, low latency and penetration in obstacles 

With good receiving antenna like the one mentioned in the receiving module it can easily pass 2km range (this is personal opinion, not taken from radiomaster website)

Refresh rate:

It refresh rate is about 1000hz this means that it can do extremely low answer delay

Gimbal(most known as joystick)

it works with hall effect, it's very common people having problems with stick drift because the mechanical piece wear itself and start getting a bit crazy, not with this.

this "joystixk" analyses electromagnetic strong force to measure movement so moving parts barely touch still parts this brings way more precision and reliability, never get a controller with no hall effect gimb it can get out of control at any time

Firmware:

it's firmware it's called EdgeTX it permits automation, calibrate things, tell what thing on the controller do each thing, LUA(Programming language) scripts, telemetry and configure such little things that you don't even think about like: curves, flying modes, and switches

Power input:

it works with two batteries li-ion 18650 that are usually not included the tension is about 6.6 to 8.4 and it has an usb-c (QC3.0) in case you don't have a battery you can power it with a power bank.

Display:

It has a pretty shit display, it's monochromatic with a 128x64 resolution just to show important and basic info in case you lost the video for example
(a video loss happens when the VTX lost signal with the googles but you can still control the drone because control and video are 2 different systems)

Controls:

It has up to 16 Channels that you can configure to do anything,


switches: 2 of 2 position and 2 momentary switch it means if you release your finger it gets back just like a ring bell

It has Trims, those are: micro adjust

and rotative buttons

Size & weight

dimensions: ~156 x 65 x 125 mm
weight:~288g

Connectivity 
Usb-C (PC simulator)
Wifi(Firmware update)
Bluetooth (PC simulator)

The RC range is much larger than the video range the video will be lost before the controls lost, this is actually good and expected because you can still control de drone to activate de GPS SOS mode 

All the info in this was taken from radiomaster website and the product's datasheet
