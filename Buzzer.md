You don't need so much, just get a generic active buzzer, it is very cheap and it will do the job, you can get it with a JST connector to make it easier to connect to your flight controller, just make sure that it is an active buzzer and not a passive one, the active buzzer will make a sound when it receives power, while the passive one needs a signal to make a sound, so just get an active buzzer and you will be good to go.
