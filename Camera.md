This is the FPV camera used in the build. It captures real-time video and sends it to the VTX, which then transmits it to the goggles.

In this project, I chose an analog camera because it is cheaper and easier to work with.
RunCam Phoenix 2 SP

- Type: Analog FPV camera
- Size: 19mm (micro)
- Voltage: 5–36V
- Aspect Ratio: 4:3 / 16:9
- Use case: freestyle / beginner
