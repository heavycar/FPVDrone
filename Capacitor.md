We need a capacitor to filter noise and reduce current spike avoiding voltage sag

What is "noise" in this context? It's basically the electrical interference that can cause instability in the drone's performance, it can be caused by bad wiring, low quality components, or even just the nature of the high current and fast switching in the drone's electronics.

What are those current spikes: When the motors suddenly require a lot of current, it can cause a sudden drop in voltage (voltage sag) which can lead to instability in flight and even crashes. The capacitor helps to smooth out these spikes by providing a temporary reserve of power.

and what about voltage sag and why current spikes cause it: Voltage sag is the drop in voltage that occurs when the drone's motors suddenly require a lot of current. This can happen when you quickly push the throttle up or when the drone is under heavy load.

The capacitor helps to mitigate voltage sag by providing a temporary reserve of power that can be drawn upon during these spikes, helping to maintain a more stable voltage and improve the drone's performance and reliability.
