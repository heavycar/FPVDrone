This is a very important part of the process and many people failure or almost do by not using it 

The name is not very suggestive but it's something that you connect between the battery and ESC that limits the current, stopping short circuits from damaging anything in the system

So in case there's a short circuit in the system your battery or ESC or FC wont't be damaged, althought it all seems ok and not shorted there could be micro contants between welds and even shorts circuits that are not exactly caused by you and caused by manufacturing process of the board

It's simple and cheap but can save you the whole system
