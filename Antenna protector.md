The antenna protector is very important for our build, many starter builders wonder where they should put the antenna and that's a really good point because depending on where you put, it can give you a very larger range and signal quality or get straight into the propeller

If you just strap it to the frame the carbon will mess up with the signal if you just leave all the antennas swinging in the back of the drone like a dog tail it will eventually hit some of the proppeler and make your drone fall

That's why we are using the antenna protector, it will assure your antenna is in in a good spot while keeping it protected and can take all types of antenna such as, ELSR T antenna(RC), VTX antenna, gps antenna and it even have a xt60 conector for the battery if you use it on the bottom so your build get's cleaner
