Ok, first if you don't know what is VTX is basically this

FPV camera - FC - OSD(THe thing that overlays the video with drone info) - Overlayed video - VTX - antenna - googles

So it's basically the thing that takes the video and send it to the googles.

I don't know if it's normal but where i am buying, it doesnt't show any board name, it only shows VTX1000 no board name but i'll try to find it out if i find i put it here

For the VTX we don't need much thing, it just needs to be analog, because our camera is, we need to locate the vtx is an strategic spot, that's why all fpv builder follow a pattern when stacking FC, Esc and vtx, the ESC always go to the bottom, FC in the middle and vtx in the top, the ESC can create unexpected eletromagnetic signal and unexpected RF signal and if it goes near VTX it can cause "noise" in the video, and even tiny lost of signal

VTX1000 photo

For the antenna we need attencion because if we put in the wrong place it can give us noise too or lost of efficiency, one error that i commited was thinking i could just strap the antenna to the frame and i would be good but the carbon most present in frames and the strap force can reduce the antenna spread signal or it's quality so you need to use an antenna holder

Here is the antenna holder that i found for Mark4 frame

Photo of antenna holder

As you can see there are 3 sma antenna holders, why 3? you won't use 3 is bacause that are some googles and some vtx that uses diversity technology, quick explanation: you use one omnidirectional antenna and one bidirectional antenna in the drone and in the googles, the bidirectional works very nice reaching ranges that omnidirectional never could but you have to point it exactly to the spot you wanna hit, the omnidirectional works good in all angles, the googles autommaticaly switches between omni and bidirectional depending on what is better. What about the third sma? knowing that if you use diversity you use two antennas you put them on both sides, that's the most logical thing to do, so the middle one is for those who actually use only one antenna so the drone won't be unbalaced

If you see on the sides there are another antenna that looks like T connected, is the RC antenna and it also have a battery connector so if you use battery on the bottom you can connect it

But the piece itself only comes with the 3d printed part and a xt60, no sma included, at least where i am buying it, so you need to buy a female SMA connector to mmcx, the mmcx you connect to the vtx if it uses mmcx like mine, and the female sma you put in the whole

So for the antenna itself i'm using a lollipop 5.8 ghz antenna it's very easy to find and buy since it just needs to be 5.8ghz and good obviously
